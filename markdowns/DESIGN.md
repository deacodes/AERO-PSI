# Design

## Visual world

An interactive editorial scientific instrument with independently composed dark and light themes. Deep ink or cool-white canvases support warm readable type, soft elevation, rounded working surfaces, and restrained blue, pink, mint, amber, coral, and cyan data marks. The collapsible left rail stays quiet while the scientific canvas uses generous negative space, orbital geometry, and varied card compositions. Low-contrast gradients are reserved for homepage module identity; run selection and scientific working surfaces remain solid and calm.

## Interaction

Navigation changes are immediate. The experience follows one traceable loop: choose evidence, inspect or model response, identify missing coverage, then interpret within the evidence boundary. The homepage has one authored orbital motion that connects those steps; it pauses offscreen and under reduced-motion preferences. Archive run cards open the selected run directly in the optical workbench, and investigation cards expose the mathematical score breakdown. Scientific graphs retain their aspect ratio, expose hover/focus values, and reveal inline actions on click or long press. AI explanations may transition to communicate a verified interpretation state.

## Interface grammar

Precise scientific page titles, a collapsible platform sidebar, elevated major work surfaces, flat internal subdivisions, custom evidence pickers, transparent derived scores, and high-contrast controls. Use a humanist sans-serif system stack headed by Avenir Next; never use Inter. No nested cards, purple, gradient text, decorative glass, floating comparison popups, or hard/zero-offset shadows.
