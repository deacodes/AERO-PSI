The problem AERO-PSI solves

Spacecraft-smoke evidence is distributed across run workbooks, housekeeping records, investigation metadata, and technical reports. AERO-PSI makes those sources explorable while keeping observations, deterministic scenarios, and unknowns scientifically separate.
